A practice, not a product.

Most of what gets called "AI for real estate" is either a wrapper around a chatbot or a venture-backed platform that asks you to abandon the systems you already trust. Neither fits how serious operators actually work.

We took a different posture. A small group of senior engineers, working in residency with a handful of CRE firms each year. We learn the moves of your asset, leasing, and acquisitions teams; we automate the parts that don't need a human in the loop; and we ship to production on the stack you already run.

The work is quieter than the marketing in our industry suggests it should be. We prefer it that way.

— the practice

Three lanes of work, all in production.

i. — Deploy

Production AI on your existing stack

Integrated with the systems your team already trusts — Yardi, MRI, Argus, VTS, RealPage, Salesforce. We build it; you own it. No vendor lock-in, no rip-and-replace.

ii. — Embed

Engineers in residence with your teams

Senior engineers sit alongside your asset managers, leasing agents, and acquisitions teams. We learn the workflow as practiced, then automate it carefully.

iii. — Steward

An ongoing relationship, not a project

We remain on as your AI operating partner. Evaluations, monitoring, new use cases as your portfolio evolves. Measured on systems live, not hours billed.

— what we ship

Concrete systems, not abstractions.

Acquisitions

Deal screening & underwriting copilots

Triage inbound deal flow against your investment criteria. Auto-extract rent rolls, T-12s, and offering memorandums into your model template.

Asset management

Lease abstraction & clause intelligence

Abstract executed leases into structured fields with citation links. Flag renewal options, escalations, exclusives, and co-tenancy triggers automatically.

Leasing

Rent-comp synthesis & market intelligence

Continuously ingest broker reports, public filings, and your internal comps to maintain a live market view by submarket and product type.

Tenant relations

Tenant communications & maintenance triage

Route, classify, and respond to tenant requests across email and portal. Escalate the 10% that need humans; handle the 90% that don't.

Portfolio operations

Variance & anomaly monitoring

Detect anomalies across budget vs. actual, occupancy, collections, and CAM reconciliation. Surface what an asset manager would catch — at portfolio scale.

— how we hold ourselves

Four operating principles.

Production or it didn't happen.

A demo is not a deliverable. We measure ourselves on systems live in your environment, used daily by your team, contributing to your numbers.

Your stack, not ours.

We integrate with Yardi, MRI, Argus, VTS, RealPage, Salesforce — the systems you already trust. We will never ask you to migrate to a platform we control.

Evaluation before scale.

Every model in production has continuous evaluations. We know when it drifts. We know what it gets wrong. We tell you before you ask.

Quiet over loud.

We don't publish hot takes about agents replacing brokers. We ship working systems, write careful documentation, and let the work compound.
