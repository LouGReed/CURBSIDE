What we do for CRE brokerages

You don't need another generic CRM. You need edge on the parts of the deal that quietly eat your week: putting together investor decks, running tours, tracking lease dates, watching the market, and showing your value when nothing's moving.

Each product below replaces something you're already doing by hand with one built around how your brokerage actually runs.

— the product suite

Four tools your brokers open every day.

1

Deck Smart

Investor decks, in days not weeks

Turn the weeks-long slog of putting together an investor deck into a few guided days. Share the site, the specifics, the story, and your brand colors. We handle the editing, formatting, color, and layout. Reclaim tens of hours and spend them where they matter: sharpening the pitch and getting stakeholders aligned.

2

Tour Guide

Match, tour, close — on the leasing side

Pulls your book and your CRM together, then matches tenants to the right properties on the spot. Produces a touring packet on your letterhead, maps the tour for convenience, and lays out the factors that close the deal for tenants weighing high-stakes leasing decisions.

3

It Won't Move

Demonstrate value when the market doesn't

Pulls exposure, inquiries, showings, outreach, and the qualified names still in the pipeline straight from your CRM and shows it as a clean one-page deal funnel. Even in market downturns, so brokers can show owners exactly what's happening, what's next, and where the real movement is hiding.

Built for the stretches when listings aren't moving.

4

Tenant HQ

Never lose a renegotiation window again

Renegotiation windows are often just 30 days — across a full book, those windows quietly disappear and so does the margin. Tenant HQ puts every critical date on one calendar, so you walk in earlier and better prepared than anyone else at the table.

Eyes on every filing, listing, sale, and ownership change in your territory — parish, ZIP, or submarket. A small set of watchers pulls the data your team would never have time to chase, so you know what's changed before anyone else does.
